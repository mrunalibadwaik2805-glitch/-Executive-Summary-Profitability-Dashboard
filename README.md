# 📊 Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, and profitability across different product categories, countries, regions, and sales channels.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through interactive visualizations, KPI indicators, and dynamic filters, helping users understand key business insights and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Track monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Evaluate gross profit margins and business profitability.
- Identify top-performing countries based on revenue.
- Analyze revenue contribution across different sales channels.
- Compare sales performance across geographical regions.
- Explore business performance using interactive filters.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue and Profit by Month
- Monthly comparison of total revenue and gross profit.
- Identifies monthly sales trends and profitability patterns.

### 2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
- Highlights the top-performing countries.
- Helps identify major revenue-generating markets.

### 3. Revenue Share by Channel
- Analyzes revenue contribution from:
  - Corporate
  - Marketplace
  - Online Store
  - Retail Partners

### 4. Region-Wise Comparison
- Compares revenue distribution across:
  - Central Europe
  - Northern Europe
  - Southern Europe
  - Western Europe

### 5. Country-Wise Map
- Geographical visualization of revenue distribution.
- Helps identify country-level business performance.

### 6. Interactive Filters (Slicers)
Users can dynamically filter dashboard insights by:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
